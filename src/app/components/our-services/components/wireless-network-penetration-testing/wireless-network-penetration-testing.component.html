What is Wireless Network Penetration Testing?

Wireless Network Penetration Testing is a proactive security assessment that mimics real-world attack scenarios on your wireless infrastructure. Our skilled security professionals employ manual and automated techniques to identify weaknesses and vulnerabilities in your wireless networks. This assessment simulates the actions of an attacker, attempting to gain unauthorized access, intercept sensitive information, or disrupt wireless communication.

Some key features include:

  • Vulnerability Identification
  • Unauthorized Access Detection
  • Encryption Assessment
  • Rogue Access Point Detection
  • Man-in-the-Middle Attacks
  • Configuration Analysis

How do we carry out Wireless Network Pen Test?

Wireless Network Penetration Testing is based on a thorough and methodical approach, combining industry best practices with cutting-edge technology and deep knowledge of network security. Our highly-skilled professionals use a systematic approach to assess the security posture of your wireless network, identify vulnerabilities and offer effective remediation strategies.

Wireless Network Map

We map your wireless network infrastructure by identifying routers, access points, controllers, and other devices to gain a clear view of your architecture and potential entry points.

Wireless Network Scan

Scanning identifies networks, SSIDs, signal strengths and encryption types. This helps assess exposed networks and weak encryption configurations.

Vulnerability Assessment

We analyze encryption protocols, password policies, segmentation and other configurations to detect weaknesses that an attacker could exploit.

Wireless Network Exploitation

We simulate realistic attack scenarios (including rogue APs, MiTM, and authentication bypass attempts) to validate real-world impact and control effectiveness.

Reporting & Recommendations

Detailed reports with reproduction steps, risk rating, and prioritized remediation guidance so your team can quickly remediate and harden the wireless environment.
